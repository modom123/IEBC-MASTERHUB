# T08 Teacher Guide: AI for Contractors and the Trades

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Self-paced video + monthly live Q&A |
| **Length** | 5 modules, about 4 hours of student time (about 60 minutes of video) |
| **Level** | Beginner |
| **Demo business** | Ridgeline Builders (3-person remodeling crew) |
| **Student deliverable** | Estimate template, job tracker and job-to-invoice workflow |

## Teaching notes

- Students work from trucks and job sites. **Demo everything on a phone too.** If it only
  works on a laptop, they won't use it.
- Keep videos short and practical. Many students will watch at lunch on site.
- ★ Pricing stays with the contractor. AI writes and formats; it doesn't set prices or
  calculate materials without the contractor checking every number.

## Course-level objectives

Students can produce a professional estimate in minutes, track jobs with photos from the
phone, take bookings with reminders, invoice on job completion and keep 1099 records.

---

## Module 1: Estimates and proposals with AI (3 videos, ~15 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Estimate template: scope, line items, exclusions, timeline, terms, signature.
2. Voice-to-estimate: record job notes on the phone → AI turns them into a scope of work.
3. ★ Mistake moment: AI adds work the contractor never mentioned ("includes permit fees") or does math wrong. Check every line and every total.
4. Exclusions protect you: ask AI to suggest exclusions, then keep only the true ones.

**SHOW:** Ridgeline bathroom remodel: 90-second voice note → scope → estimate PDF.
**Assignment 1:** Estimate template + one sample estimate from a real (or recent) job.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Missing exclusions/terms or math errors | Complete template, correct totals | Also an e-signature step and deposit terms |

## Module 2: Jobs from your phone (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH: job tracker (status, address, customer, photos, notes), before/during/after photo routine, materials and supply cost log per job, and getting photos off the camera roll and into the job record.

## Module 3: Booking and customer follow-up (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: online booking for estimates, appointment reminders, "on my way" texts, and review requests after completion (T02 pattern, trades examples).

## Module 4: Invoices and payments (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: invoice from the estimate when the job is marked complete, deposits and progress billing, payment reminders (friendly → firm → call).

## Module 5: Taxes and 1099 records (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: track expenses per job, W-9 before paying subs, keep sub payments recorded. Disclaimer: tax decisions belong with a tax professional.

**Assignment 2 (final):** Job-to-invoice workflow working end to end on one real or test job.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Steps still manual or untested | Estimate → job → invoice flows with photos attached | Also review request and payment reminders on |

## Live Q&A focus

"Estimate clinic": students share (redacted) estimates; teacher improves scope wording and exclusions live.
