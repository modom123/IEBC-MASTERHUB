# T09 Teacher Guide: AI for Creatives and Makers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Self-paced video + monthly live Q&A |
| **Length** | 5 modules, about 4 hours of student time (about 60 minutes of video) |
| **Level** | Beginner |
| **Demo business** | Willow & Kiln Studio (solo ceramic artist) |
| **Case study** | Ceces Pieces, using only what's published on the IEBC website |
| **Student deliverable** | Storefront plan, commission workflow and a sent newsletter |

## Teaching notes

- ★ Be sensitive about AI and art. Many artists have strong feelings about AI-generated
  images. This course uses AI for the **business** around the art (writing, organizing,
  marketing, admin), not to make the art. Say this in the first minute.
- Students' time is their art. Emphasize systems that free time for making.

## Course-level objectives

Students can set up an online storefront with deposits for commissions, run commissions
from inquiry to delivery, track inventory and income, sell classes or rentals, and keep
marketing going with a newsletter and scheduled posts.

---

## Module 1: Your online storefront (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH: gallery and series structure, product pages (story, size, materials, care, shipping), Buy Now checkout using a payment platform, and writing product descriptions with AI from the artist's own notes (★ never let AI invent materials or techniques).

## Module 2: Commissions from inquiry to delivery (3 videos, ~15 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Commission intake form: what, size, budget range, deadline, references, how they found you.
2. Deposit before starting; written timeline and approval points (sketch → progress photo → final).
3. Terms: revisions included, cancellation, shipping.
4. AI drafts the commission agreement summary and status updates; the artist approves.

**Assignment 1:** Commission intake form + terms + status-update templates.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| No deposit or terms | Form, deposit, timeline and terms in place | Also automated status updates at each approval point |

## Module 3: Inventory and income (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: inventory with photos and status (available, on hold, sold, on loan/rented, at gallery), income by type (originals, prints, commissions, classes, rentals), and knowing which type actually pays.

## Module 4: Classes, rentals and bookings (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: selling classes and workshops with capacity limits, rental bookings with deposits and condition notes, reminders before each booking.

## Module 5: Marketing on autopilot (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH: studio newsletter (one new piece, one behind-the-scenes, one upcoming event), social posts generated from each new piece (T06 method), consent before posting buyers' homes or faces.

**Assignment 2 (final):** First newsletter sent + storefront plan.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Not sent | Newsletter sent; storefront plan with categories and checkout | Storefront live with at least 5 pieces |

## Live Q&A focus

Portfolio-to-product clinic: turn 3 student pieces into product pages live.
