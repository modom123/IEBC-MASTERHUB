# T14 Teacher Guide: AI Readiness Workshop for Teams

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Half day (4 hours), on site or live video |
| **Group size** | Up to 25 (Producer/co-facilitator required) |
| **Level** | All levels, mixed roles |
| **Demo example** | Brightpath Dental Group, then the client's own work |
| **Client deliverable** | A written AI Opportunity Report and action plan (delivered within 5 business days) |

## Before the workshop (client prep)

1. **Kickoff call with the client's sponsor (30 min), 2+ weeks before:**
   - Departments and roles attending; what tools they already use; any AI policy today
   - Data sensitivity (health, financial, children's data?) → sets the safety examples
   - What success looks like for the sponsor
   - Approved AI tool(s) for the hands-on part. ★ If the company has no approved tool, use
     demo data only and say so to attendees.
2. **Pre-survey to attendees (5 questions):** role, current AI use, top 3 repetitive tasks, comfort level (1–5), one worry.
3. Prepare: department-specific examples (2 per department) from the survey answers.

## Learning objectives

Attendees can: use AI for at least two real tasks in their job, follow the company's
data rules, and identify and rank automation opportunities in their department.

## Run of show (4 hours)

| Time | Segment | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Welcome; sponsor opens (2 min); goals; survey highlights (comfort levels, top worry) | |
| 0:20–0:50 | AI basics: what it's good and bad at; ★ mistake moment; ★ data rules | SHOW |
| 0:50–1:30 | Hands-on prompting with their own tasks (department tables) | TRY → BUILD |
| 1:30–1:45 | Break | |
| 1:45–2:15 | Safe use: what never goes into AI tools; checking output before it goes out; scenarios | SHOW → TRY |
| 2:15–3:05 | Opportunity mapping: department time audits and scoring | BUILD |
| 3:05–3:15 | Break | |
| 3:15–3:45 | Action plan: each department presents its top 5 quick wins with an owner and date | SHARE |
| 3:45–4:00 | Close: what happens next, survey | |

### Segment notes

**AI basics (★):** assistant vs. automation vs. agent; reliable vs. needs-a-human; a live demo
in their industry (Brightpath: summarize a long insurance policy update into 5 bullets for
front-desk staff, and show the AI misreading one detail).

**Hands-on prompting (★):** each attendee brings one real task (de-identified). 4-part prompt
template on a printed card. Facilitators circulate. Goal: every attendee gets one usable output.
Stretch: build a prompt their whole team could reuse.

**Safe use (★):** use the client's policy if it exists; otherwise the IEBC baseline rules
(Playbook §7). Scenarios in small groups: *"A patient emails asking about a bill. Can you paste
the email into the AI tool?"* Groups decide, then discuss. Bring 6 scenarios tailored to the client.

**Opportunity mapping (★):** department tables list tasks, score them (frequency × time ×
frustration, plus risk as a filter), and pick the top 5. Facilitators push for specific,
repetitive, low-risk tasks.

## Handling a mixed room

- Seat by department, but put one confident AI user at each table.
- Address fear directly in the first 20 minutes: AI is for removing tedious work; leadership
  has committed to (whatever the sponsor confirmed in the kickoff call).
- Leaders participate as attendees, not observers. Ask the sponsor in advance.

## After the workshop

Deliver the **AI Opportunity Report** within 5 business days:
1. Summary (one page): attendance, pre/post comfort level, top themes.
2. Opportunities by department, ranked, with effort/impact.
3. Top 5 company-wide quick wins with owners and dates.
4. Safety and policy recommendations (if no policy exists, recommend T16).
5. Suggested next steps (for example, a department bootcamp, T15). ★ Recommendations must
   follow from what the workshop found, not from what IEBC sells.

## Facilitator scoring (for internal quality)

- Every attendee produced at least one usable AI output: yes/no
- Every department left with a top-5 list with owners: yes/no
- Post-survey average ≥ 4/5 and comfort level up by ≥ 1 point
