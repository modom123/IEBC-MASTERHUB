# T05 Teacher Guide: Build a Website That Sells, with AI

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Self-paced video course + monthly live Q&A and site reviews |
| **Length** | 6 modules, about 5 hours of student time (about 75 minutes of video) |
| **Level** | Beginner |
| **Demo business** | Lakeside Home Services |
| **Student deliverable** | A live website with booking or payments and lead capture |

## Teaching notes

- Choose **one** beginner-friendly website builder for the recordings and say why. Students
  using another builder can follow the same steps; the patterns are identical.
- The hardest part for students is not the tool. It's knowing what to say. Spend the most
  energy on Modules 1–2.
- ★ Honesty: if a student needs a complex site (many products, custom systems), say so and
  mention that IEBC builds those. Don't pretend a one-weekend site does everything.

## Course-level objectives

Students can plan, write, design, connect and launch a 5-page service website that turns
visitors into inquiries, and measure whether it's working.

---

## Module 1: Plan a site that converts (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. The 5 pages: Home, Services, About, Reviews/Work, Contact/Book.
2. The one-sentence offer: *"We help [who] get [result] in [where], without [pain]."*
3. One main call to action, repeated on every page.
4. The 5-second test: a stranger should know what you do and what to click within 5 seconds.

**SHOW:** Write Lakeside's offer sentence (three drafts, pick one) and sketch its pages on paper.
**BUILD:** Students write their offer sentence and page list.
**Watch for:** Offers that list everything ("plumbing, HVAC, remodeling, handyman, consulting"). Coach toward the most profitable or most requested service first.

## Module 2: Writing copy with AI (3 videos, ~15 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Feed AI the facts first: services, service area, prices or price ranges, guarantees, 3 real customer comments. AI must not invent these.
2. ★ Mistake moment: show AI inventing "Licensed in 3 states, 25 years experience, 100% satisfaction guarantee." Delete every claim that isn't true.
3. Headlines: ask for 10, pick 1.
4. FAQs from real questions customers ask.
5. Read it out loud; if it doesn't sound like you, rewrite it.

**SHOW:** Lakeside home page and services page copy.
**BUILD:** Students draft their Home and Services pages.

**Assignment 1:** Home and Services copy.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Contains unverified claims or no clear call to action | Clear offer, true claims, one call to action | Includes real customer quotes and answers 3+ common questions |

## Module 3: Design and images (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Pick a template close to the end result; change less, not more.
2. Brand basics: 2 colors, 2 fonts, a simple logo (text logos are fine).
3. Real photos beat stock photos. Phone photos of real work, in good light.
4. AI images: fine for backgrounds and illustrations; ★ never use AI images to show work you didn't do or people who aren't your team. Check the license terms of the image tool.
5. Mobile first: check every page on a phone before publishing.

## Module 4: Booking, payments and lead capture (3 videos, ~15 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Online booking (built-in or embedded scheduler) with buffers and working hours.
2. Payments and deposits: use the builder's or payment processor's built-in tools; never collect card numbers in a plain form.
3. Lead form: few fields (name, contact, what they need); send to email and a spreadsheet or CRM.
4. Instant auto-reply (link to T02's follow-up pattern).

**SHOW:** Lakeside "Book a visit" and "Request a quote" with auto-reply.
**BUILD:** Students add their booking or form and test it 3 times.

## Module 5: Getting found (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH: Google Business Profile, page titles and descriptions with the service + city, asking every happy customer for a review (with the T02 review-request automation).

## Module 6: Launch and improve (2 videos, ~10 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Launch checklist: domain connected, every link works, forms tested, mobile checked, contact info correct, privacy page if collecting data.
2. Read the basic numbers monthly: visitors, inquiries, inquiry rate.
3. One improvement per month.

**Assignment 2 (final):** Live site link submitted for an IEBC review.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Form or booking doesn't work, or fails the 5-second test | Live, mobile-friendly, working form/booking, passes 5-second test | Also Google Business Profile and analytics set up |

**Site review standard (reviewers):** use the launch checklist plus the 5-second test with
someone who's never seen the site. Feedback format per Playbook §9.

## Monthly live session: "Site Review Clinic" (60 min)

Review 4–6 student sites live (with permission): 5-second test, mobile check, form test,
one improvement each. Students learn as much from watching others' reviews as their own.
