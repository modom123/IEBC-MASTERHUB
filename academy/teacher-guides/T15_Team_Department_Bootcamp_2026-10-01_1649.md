# T15 Teacher Guide: AI at Work: Department Bootcamp

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 4–6 weekly sessions (90 min) per department, on site or live video |
| **Group size** | One department, up to 15 |
| **Level** | All levels |
| **Client deliverable** | Working automations, a shared prompt library, a time-saved report and trained internal champions |

## Structure

The bootcamp is the same four phases for every department. Content changes by department;
the method doesn't.

| Phase | Weeks | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Before week 1 | Interview the department lead + 3 staff (30 min each). Pick 2–4 target workflows. Agree on how time will be measured. |
| 2. Skills | Week 1–2 | Prompting for the department's real work; shared templates and a prompt library |
| 3. Build | Week 3–4 (to 5) | Build 2–4 automations with the team on their real workflows |
| 4. Measure and hand off | Final week | Before/after time, train 1–2 internal champions, handoff documents |

## Department playbooks (examples to adapt)

| Department | Typical target workflows | Must-teach safety point |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | Lead research summaries, follow-up drafting, call notes → CRM, proposal first drafts | No customer personal data in unapproved tools; pricing comes from the price book |
| **Finance** | Invoice processing, expense categorization, month-end checklists, variance explanations | AI never posts entries or moves money; two-person review |
| **Operations** | SOP drafting, scheduling, inventory alerts, vendor emails | Changes to SOPs approved by the process owner |
| **HR** | Job descriptions, onboarding checklists, policy FAQs, interview question banks | No AI screening or ranking of candidates without legal review; no employee personal data |
| **Customer service** | Reply drafts, knowledge-base articles, ticket tagging and summaries | Human sends anything with refunds, promises or complaints |

## Weekly session template (90 min)

Use the Playbook timeline (§6.2). Week-specific content:

- **Week 1 (Skills):** the 4-part prompt with the department's real tasks; ★ mistake moment
  using their kind of document; ★ the company's data rules. Each person builds 3 prompts.
- **Week 2 (Skills):** shared prompt library: the team agrees on 10 standard prompts, names
  and owners. ◆ Templates in the tools they already use.
- **Week 3–4 (Build):** one target workflow per session. SHOW the design, TRY on a test copy,
  BUILD on the real workflow with the process owner present. ★ 3 test runs before going live.
- **Final week:** measure before/after; champions practice teaching one workflow to a
  colleague using the Build-Along arc; handoff.

## Internal champions

Pick 1–2 per department (volunteers with interest, not just the most technical). In the final
week they: (1) run a 10-minute teach-back, (2) receive the handoff document, (3) get a
30-day check-in call with the teacher.

## Handoff document (per department)

- Each automation: what it does, trigger, owner, how to turn it off, how to fix common failures
- The prompt library with owners
- Before/after time measures and how to keep measuring
- Weekly 10-minute check routine

## Success criteria

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Automations live and owned | 2–4 per department |
| Staff using the prompt library weekly (30-day check) | ≥ 70% |
| Measured time saved | Reported honestly, whatever it is |
| Post-survey | ≥ 4/5 |
