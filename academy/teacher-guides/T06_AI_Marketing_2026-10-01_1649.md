# T06 Teacher Guide: AI Marketing and Social Content Engine

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Self-paced video course + monthly live Q&A |
| **Length** | 5 modules, about 4 hours of student time (about 60 minutes of video) |
| **Level** | Beginner |
| **Demo business** | Lakeside Home Services |
| **Student deliverable** | A scheduled 30-day content calendar and a newsletter template |

## Teaching notes

- Students often think the problem is "I don't know what to post." The real problem is
  having no system. Teach the system: voice guide → pillars → one idea into many pieces → batch → schedule → review.
- ★ Content rules to repeat: no fake reviews or testimonials, no claims you can't back up,
  get permission before posting customers' photos or names, and follow each platform's rules
  for ads and promotions.

## Course-level objectives

Students can capture their brand voice so AI matches it, plan content pillars, turn one
idea into 10 pieces, grow and send a newsletter, and schedule a month of posts in one sitting.

---

## Module 1: Your brand voice in a prompt (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. The voice guide: 3 adjectives, words you use, words you never use, 3 sample posts you like.
2. Content pillars: 3–5 recurring themes (for Lakeside: tips, before/after jobs, team, seasonal reminders, offers).
3. The 80/20 rule: 80% helpful, 20% promotional.

**BUILD:** Students write their voice guide and pillars.

## Module 2: One idea, ten pieces (3 videos, ~15 min)

★ MUST TEACH:
1. Start from something real: a job, a customer question, a mistake you see often.
2. The repurpose prompt: give AI the story + voice guide, ask for 10 pieces (3 posts, 1 short video script, 1 email, 1 blog outline, 2 captions, 1 FAQ answer, 1 offer post).
3. ★ Mistake moment: AI adds "Call now, only 2 spots left!" when it isn't true. Delete false urgency.
4. Edit pass: cut 30%, add one specific detail only you know.

**SHOW:** Lakeside's frozen-pipe call turned into 10 pieces.
**Assignment 1:** One real story turned into 10 pieces, edited.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Unedited AI output or false claims | 10 edited pieces in the student's voice | Includes a short video script they actually filmed |

## Module 3: Email and newsletters (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH: grow the list with consent (website signup, in-person ask), a simple monthly format (one story, one tip, one offer), subject lines (ask AI for 10, pick 1), and unsubscribe/consent rules: use an email platform that handles them.

## Module 4: Scheduling and automation (2 videos, ~12 min)

★ MUST TEACH: batch creation (one sitting per month), a scheduling tool, auto-posting when something new happens (new product, finished job), and leaving room for real-time posts.

## Module 5: Measure and improve (1 video, ~8 min)

★ MUST TEACH: the few numbers that matter (reach, saves/shares, clicks, inquiries from social), a monthly 20-minute review, and doing more of what worked.

**Assignment 2 (final):** Screenshot of 30 days scheduled + newsletter template.

| Not yet | Meets | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Fewer than 12 posts scheduled | 30 days planned with at least 12 posts scheduled + template | Also first newsletter sent and a review date set |

## Monthly live session

"Content Clinic": students bring one draft post or email; teacher improves 6–8 live using the
voice guide method.
